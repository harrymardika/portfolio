## Problem
Making a clear, good-looking presentation takes hours, and most people start from a blank slide or an outdated template.

## Approach
Built an AI-powered SaaS platform that generates complete decks from plain text, uploaded documents, or legacy PPTX files. Stack: Next.js frontend, Go backend, PostgreSQL. Led a 5-person team funded by a national PKM-K grant (IDR 5.85M).

## Result
- Launched commercially at decklify.id within 3 months of starting.
- +45 NPS and 8.39/10 satisfaction score; 100+ decks generated for 50+ users in the first 2 weeks.
- Business model with an 83.68% contribution margin and break-even at 11 transactions per month.
